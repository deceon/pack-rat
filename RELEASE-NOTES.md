# Strongbox 1.0.0

First release: know where everything is.

- Looks up every item name it knows (your recipes, your bag, what you pick up, your Crafters Ledger list and names you add) in the background, one at a time, and saves what it finds.
- Items page: everything you own, searchable by any words and sorted by name, amount or places, with each place's total and containers.
- Chests page: every bag, bank container and house chest that holds something, grouped by place, with its contents.
- Scattered page: items spread across more than one chest or bank container.
- Mark a chest once and Compass points the game's compass at it; name your containers. Marks and names from Crafters Ledger come across.
